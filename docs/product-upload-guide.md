# Uploading products with real photos

Everything you need to sign in and start adding real products — with real photos — to the store. Takes about two minutes per product once you've done your first one.

✓

**Before you start** Ask whoever set up the system for your login email and password. There's no public sign-up — accounts are created one at a time, on purpose, so random people can't get into the store's data.

## Step by step

1

### Sign in

Open the admin link you were given and sign in with your email and password. You'll land on the Dashboard, showing sales and orders at a glance.

2

### Check the category exists

In the left sidebar, go to **Categories**. Every product needs to belong to one (Gadgets, Fashion, Accessories, etc). If the category you need isn't there yet, click **Add category** and create it first — takes a few seconds.

3

### Start a new product

Go to **Products** in the sidebar, then click **Add product** (top right).

4

### Fill in the basics

Product name, a short description, category, sub-category and brand.

- **SKU** — leave this blank. It's generated automatically from the category and product name.

5

### Upload the photos

Click the **Upload images** tile and select as many photos as you want — there's no limit on how many.

- The **first photo you add becomes the cover image** — the one customers see first. Add your best shot first.
- JPG or PNG both work.
- You can remove any photo before saving by hovering it and tapping the ✕.

6

### Set the price

Enter the price in naira (just the number, e.g. `64000` for ₦64,000). Add a sale price only if it's currently discounted — leave it blank otherwise.

7

### TracPay (optional)

If this product can be paid for over time, leave **Pay with TracPay** checked and choose which plans apply (3 / 6 / 12 months). Turn it off for products that must be paid in full.

8

### Set the stock count

Enter how many units you have. The "alert me when stock is below" number controls when it shows up as low stock on the dashboard — 5 is a sensible default.

9

### Save it

Two buttons, top right of the form:

- **Save as draft** — stores it, but customers can't see it yet. Good for "still working on this one."
- **Publish** — goes live immediately.

---

!

**Photo tips worth following** Square-ish photos on a plain background, good lighting, no visible watermarks from other sites. Products with clear, honest photos sell better — and customers compare against what actually arrives.
